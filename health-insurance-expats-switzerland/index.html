🏥 Health Insurance · Guide for expats

Health insurance for expats in Switzerland (2027)

What newcomers need to know about Swiss basic health insurance: who has to be insured, the three-month deadline, retroactive cover, your share of the costs and how to compare premiums.

Who is this for? People who are moving or have just moved to Switzerland, and expats who are already insured and thinking about switching for 2027.
General information, not legal advice. Your canton and your insurer decide on individual cases.

Do I have to take out Swiss health insurance as an expat?

Yes, if you live in Switzerland. Basic health insurance is mandatory for everyone resident in Switzerland, whatever their nationality, and every family member, children included, is insured individually. There are exceptions, for example for certain cross-border workers, students and posted workers. An exemption only applies once the competent canton has approved it; an application alone is not enough. If you think you are a special case, clarify it with your canton.

How long do I have to take out insurance after moving to Switzerland?

You must register with a basic health insurer within three months of moving to Switzerland.

From when am I covered if I register on time?

If you register within the three months, your cover starts when your insurance obligation began. Premiums are owed retroactively from that date, calculated to the day.

Can an insurer turn me down because of my age or health?

No. Every insurer offering basic insurance where you live must accept you, regardless of age or state of health, without reservations and without a waiting period.

How much do I pay myself when I need treatment?

On top of the premium you pay an annual deductible (franchise): CHF 300 to 2,500 for adults and CHF 0 to 600 for children. After that you normally pay a 10% retention, with exceptions set by law, up to a maximum of CHF 700 a year for adults and CHF 350 for children. The higher the deductible you choose, the lower your premium. These are the main elements of cost-sharing, not a complete list of every cost you may have to pay yourself.

I am already insured. How do I switch insurer for 2027?

Your cancellation must reach your current insurer by 30 November 2026. To be safe, send it by registered mail or A-Post Plus by 15 November and register with your new insurer at the same time. This deadline is for people who are already insured; do not confuse it with the three-month period for newcomers.

How do I compare premiums?

Use the official premium calculator of the federal government on priminfo.admin.ch. It shows the premiums for your postcode, your deductible and the insurance model you choose.
