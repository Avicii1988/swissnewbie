Apps & ticketing
🚆

SBB Mobile app

The most-used app in Switzerland. Buy tickets, check real-time delays, platform numbers, and manage your Halbtax/GA. Essential — download on arrival.

📲

FAIRTIQ

Tap to start, tap to stop — the app automatically calculates the cheapest fare for your trip. No need to look up routes or prices. Works on all Swiss public transport.

🚲

PubliBike

Swiss bike sharing network — available in most cities. CHF 3.50/30min or day passes. SBB app integrates PubliBike for door-to-station connections.

Frequently asked questions
Should I buy a Halbtax or a GA? ▾

Start with Halbtax (CHF 185/year). Track your ticket spending for 1–2 months. If you consistently spend more than CHF 357/month on tickets even with Halbtax, a GA makes financial sense. Most people, especially in cities with a good local zone subscription, are better off with Halbtax + zone subscription.

Can I use public transport without a ticket if I have a GA? ▾

Yes — your GA is your ticket for all SBB trains, PostBus, and urban transport (trams, buses) in all zones. You just need to scan your GA card or show the app. You don't buy separate tickets. Some mountain railways and special trains may require a surcharge even with GA.

Are Swiss trains really on time? ▾

Nearly — SBB reports ~93% of trains arriving within 3 minutes of schedule. Connections of under 5 minutes are common and usually work. However, major delays do occur, especially in winter storms. The SBB app gives real-time delay info and will automatically rebook you if you miss a connection.

Do I need a car in Switzerland? ▾

In cities: definitely not. Public transport covers everything. In rural areas or smaller towns: a car helps but is rarely essential — there are bus routes to almost every village. Many Swiss families use Mobility car sharing for the occasional big shopping trip or weekend excursion and avoid car ownership entirely.
