Public Holidays & School Holidays in Switzerland 2027

In Switzerland, public holidays are mostly set by the cantons — only 1 August is a federal holiday. This guide lists the 2027 holidays with exact dates, shows where each one applies, explains how school holidays work and points out the best bridge days for long weekends.

Who is this for? Expats who want to plan work, travel and childcare around Swiss holidays in 2027.

Holidays on a weekend in 2027

Several holidays fall on a Saturday or Sunday in 2027:

  • Berchtold's Day — Sat 2 January 2027
  • Labour Day — Sat 1 May 2027
  • Swiss National Day — Sun 1 August 2027
  • Assumption Day — Sun 15 August 2027
  • Christmas Day — Sat 25 December 2027
  • St Stephen's Day — Sun 26 December 2027

In Switzerland, holidays that fall on a weekend are generally not moved to a weekday — so 2027 has fewer extra days off than other years.

Why holidays differ by canton

Only 1 August, Swiss National Day, is a federal public holiday. It is legally equated with a Sunday and must be paid. All other holidays are set by the cantons, which is why the list depends on where you work:

  • Catholic tradition: cantons such as Lucerne, Ticino and Valais also observe Corpus Christi, Assumption, All Saints' Day and the Immaculate Conception.
  • Own holidays: Geneva has the Jeûne genevois and the Restoration of the Republic, Vaud the Federal Fast Monday, Glarus the Näfels Festival.
  • Labour Day: a public holiday only in some cantons, e.g. Zurich and Basel-Stadt.

If you live in one canton and work in another, the holidays of your place of work usually apply. Check your employment contract and your employer's holiday calendar.

School holidays 2027

School holidays are set by the cantons and, in some cantons, by the municipalities. Exact dates differ, but the pattern is similar across Switzerland:

HolidaysTypical timing
Christmas & New Year
About two weeks around the turn of the year
Sport holidays
One to two weeks in February or early March, staggered by canton
Spring holidays
About two weeks in spring, often in April; with Easter as early as 28 March in 2027, not every canton aligns them with Easter
Summer holidays
German-speaking cantons: around five to six weeks from early or mid-July. French-speaking cantons: often longer, into late August. Ticino: the longest summer break, usually from mid-June
Autumn holidays
Two to three weeks in late September or October

Always check the official school calendar of your canton or your child's school before booking travel — dates can differ even between neighbouring municipalities.

Bridge days and long weekends 2027

  • Easter: Good Friday (Fri 26 March 2027) to Easter Monday (Mon 29 March 2027) gives four days off. Taking the four working days after it gives ten days off in a row, until Sun 4 April 2027.
  • Ascension: Thu 6 May 2027 — take Friday Fri 7 May 2027 off for a four-day weekend.
  • Whitsun: Whit Monday (Mon 17 May 2027) — with four days off from Tue 18 May 2027 to Fri 21 May 2027 you get nine days off in a row.
  • Corpus Christi (where observed): Thu 27 May 2027 — take Friday Fri 28 May 2027 off for a four-day weekend.
  • Geneva: Jeûne genevois on Thu 9 September 2027 — take Friday Fri 10 September 2027 off. Vaud: Federal Fast Monday on Mon 20 September 2027 makes a long weekend.
  • All Saints' Day (where observed): Mon 1 November 2027 makes a long weekend.
  • Year end: Christmas falls on a weekend. Taking Mon 27 December 2027 to Fri 31 December 2027 off (five working days; four in Geneva, where 31 December is a holiday) gives nine days off until Sun 2 January 2028.

School holiday weeks are the busiest and most expensive travel periods — book early or travel just outside them. Our annual savings check and the vouchers page help you cut other costs.

Frequently asked questions

When is Easter 2027 in Switzerland? ▾

Easter Sunday is on 28 March 2027. Good Friday is on 26 March and Easter Monday on 29 March; in Ticino and Valais, Good Friday is not a public holiday.

Which public holidays apply everywhere in Switzerland? ▾

Only 1 August, Swiss National Day, is a federal public holiday. New Year’s Day, Ascension Day and Christmas Day are observed in all cantons; all other holidays depend on the canton.

Is 1 May a public holiday in Switzerland? ▾

Only in some cantons, for example Zurich and Basel-Stadt. In 2027, 1 May falls on a Saturday.

Are holidays that fall on a weekend moved in Switzerland? ▾

Generally no. In 2027, holidays such as 1 August and Christmas Day fall on a weekend and are not replaced by a weekday.

When are the school holidays 2027? ▾

They are set by the cantons and in some cases by the municipalities. Typical periods are sport holidays in February or March, spring holidays, summer holidays from late June or July and autumn holidays — check your canton’s school calendar for exact dates.
