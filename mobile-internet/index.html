TV streaming tip

Zattoo is the most popular Swiss TV streaming app — covers all Swiss public channels (SRF, RTS, RSI) plus German/international channels. Much cheaper than a cable TV package.

Cross-border residents

Living in Basel, Geneva, or Schaffhausen? EU roaming is included with most Swiss plans when you cross the border, but check your exact plan — MVNO plans often restrict roaming days.

Frequently asked questions
Which SIM to get on day one? ▾

If you want value immediately, get a Sunrise or Salt SIM from a convenience store. Spusu (Salt network) offers great cheap prepaid. For a proper monthly contract with unlimited data, use the SwissNewbie Sunrise link — up to 50% off normal prices.

Can I use my home country SIM when I arrive? ▾

Yes, EU roaming rules apply for EU SIMs. However charges can add up quickly. Most people switch to a local Swiss SIM within a week or two of arriving.

Do Swiss phone plans include EU roaming? ▾

Most main network plans (Swisscom, Sunrise, Salt) include EU roaming. MVNO plans (Wingo, Yallo, Spusu) often include limited EU data — check the exact amount. Switzerland is not in the EU, so roaming agreements are bilateral and vary by provider.

Swisscom vs Sunrise — which is better? ▾

Swisscom has marginally better rural coverage (important if you hike or travel to the Alps), but costs 30–50% more. Sunrise covers 99%+ of populated areas and is significantly cheaper, especially with SwissNewbie's deal. For city dwellers, Sunrise is the clear winner on value.
