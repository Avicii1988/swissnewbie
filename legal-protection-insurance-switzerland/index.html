Legal Protection Insurance
in Switzerland

This guide is for anyone considering legal protection insurance in Switzerland — whether as a newcomer or an established resident.

General information only — not legal or insurance advice. Coverage and conditions vary significantly between providers. Always read the full policy terms (AVB/CGA) before purchasing. No commercial partner exists for this category — this guide is fully independent.

What legal protection insurance covers (and what it doesn't)
Legal protection insurance covers defined legal costs in certain areas. Scope varies between policies — always check the full terms.
✅

Typically covered

Tenancy: disputes with landlords
Employment: disputes with employers
Traffic/vehicle: accidents, traffic violations
Private/consumer: general private and consumer disputes
Coverage details vary — always check the specific policy.

🚫

Often NOT covered by standard policies

Tax disputes (usually excluded)
Divorce and family law (sometimes available as add-on)
Criminal defence by choice
Disputes related to professional activities
The policy document (AVB/CGA) is the only reliable source.

⚠️
Coverage areas and exclusions vary significantly between insurers and policies. Never rely on the policy name alone — read the full terms (AVB/CGA).
Common situations where it may be relevant
These are examples only — not a recommendation to purchase insurance.
🏠
Tenancy: deposit disputes, repairs a landlord refuses to make, lease termination disputes.
💼
Employment: disagreements over salary, dismissal, working conditions.
🚗
Traffic: disagreements after an accident about liability or damage.
🛒
Consumer: disputes with a service provider or product manufacturer.
Key things to check in any policy
Use this checklist when comparing legal protection insurance policies.
Waiting period
Wartezeit / délai d'attente — typically around 3 months. Claims for disputes that begin during or before this period are excluded.
Pre-existing disputes
Excluded in virtually all policies. Disputes that already exist before the policy starts are not covered.
Deductible per claim
Selbstbehalt / franchise — check if there is a co-payment per case.
Free choice of lawyer
Verify whether you can choose your own lawyer or are limited to the insurer's network.
Geographic coverage
Switzerland only? EU/Europe? Worldwide? Check what is included.
Specific exclusions
Tax, divorce, criminal, professional activities — check what is excluded for your specific situation.
Maximum coverage per case
There is usually a cap on covered legal costs per claim.
Mediation/arbitration
Some policies require a mediation or arbitration step before litigation.
How to decide if it makes sense for you
Not everyone needs legal protection insurance. Use this framework to think through your situation.
🏠
Renting in Switzerland and not a member of a tenant association or union: a tenancy module may be worth considering, as tenancy disputes are common.
💼
Employed and not a union member: an employment module may add value for disputes over dismissal or working conditions.
🚗
Own a vehicle: a traffic module is often considered relevant by vehicle owners.
🤝
Already have tenant association or union legal support: check for overlap before purchasing — you may already have legal coverage for tenancy or employment disputes.
🎓
Student or very early in career: lower risk exposure in some areas — consider whether the cost is justified for your situation.
💡
If you already belong to a tenant association or trade union that provides legal support, there may be overlap. Evaluate your own risk profile and existing coverage before purchasing.
How to compare policies
Use official or independent comparison tools and always read the full policy terms.
  • comparis.ch — commercial comparison tool (check their independence disclosure)
  • moneyland.ch — commercial comparison tool
  • Always read the full AVB/CGA — not just the summary or marketing materials
  • Ask specifically about exclusions relevant to your situation before purchasing

Frequently asked questions
What does legal protection insurance cover in Switzerland? ▾

Legal protection insurance typically covers lawyer fees, court costs and expert witnesses in defined areas such as tenancy disputes, employment disagreements, traffic incidents and general private disputes. Coverage varies significantly between policies — the policy terms (AVB/CGA) are the only reliable source.

What is a waiting period in legal protection insurance? ▾

Most legal protection insurance policies in Switzerland have a waiting period (Wartezeit / délai d'attente), typically around 3 months, before you can make a claim. Any dispute that begins before or during this waiting period is excluded. This is a standard exclusion across virtually all policies.

Does legal protection insurance cover divorce and family law? ▾

Standard legal protection insurance policies typically exclude divorce and most family law matters. Some insurers offer family law as an optional add-on. Always check the specific policy terms (AVB/CGA) for what is and is not included.

How do I compare legal protection insurance policies in Switzerland? ▾

Commercial comparison tools such as comparis.ch and moneyland.ch list legal protection insurance options. Always read the full policy terms (AVB/CGA) and ask specifically about exclusions relevant to your situation before purchasing. There is no SwissNewbie affiliate partner for this category.
