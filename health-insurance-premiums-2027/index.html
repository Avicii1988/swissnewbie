🏥 Health insurance · Premiums 2027

Health insurance premiums 2027 by canton

The average health insurance premium in Switzerland rises by 5.0 % to CHF 412 per month in 2027. The Federal Office of Public Health (FOPH) published this on 29 September 2026. How much premiums rise depends heavily on your canton of residence. Here you will find the official averages for five large cantons – and everything you need to know to switch by the end of November.

Deadlines for switching

  • Cancellation: Your cancellation of basic insurance must reach your health insurer by 30 November 2026 at the latest. The FOPH recommends sending the written cancellation by 15 November by registered mail or A-Post Plus.
  • Registration: Sign up with the new insurer at the same time – do not wait for the cancellation confirmation. The new insurer confirms the switch in writing, to you and to your previous insurer.
  • Effective from: The switch takes effect on 1 January 2027.

Source: FOPH (in German)

Compare premiums

You can find the official comparison of all health insurers in your municipality in the Priminfo premium calculator, the federal government’s portal.

Frequently asked questions

How much will health insurance premiums rise in 2027? ▾

On the Swiss average by 5.0 % to CHF 412 per month. For adults, the average monthly premium rises by CHF 23 (4.9 %) to CHF 488. The increase varies by canton.

When is the deadline to switch health insurer for 2027? ▾

Your cancellation must reach your health insurer by 30 November 2026 at the latest. The FOPH recommends sending the written cancellation by 15 November by registered mail or A-Post Plus. The switch takes effect on 1 January 2027.

Why do premiums differ by canton? ▾

Premiums must cover the expected costs in each canton. What counts is where you live; some cantons are also divided into premium regions. Your actual premium also depends on age, deductible, insurer and insurance model.
